<blockquote>
<p>In Federalist no. 51, James Madison, himself an avid student of politics, observed that, “in framing a government which is to be administered by men over men, the great difficulty lies in this: you must first enable the government to control the governed; and in the next place oblige it to control itself.”</p>
<footer class="attribution">James Madison, Federalist No. 51</footer>
