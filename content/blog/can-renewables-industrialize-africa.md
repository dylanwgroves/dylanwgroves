> The challenge for African governments is not to replace one form of dependence with another. It must use foreign investment to expand domestic capacity, building up local manufacturing and strengthening regional power networks. Sub-Saharan Africa is already the world's leading market for pay-as-you-go-solar. If its transition is managed well, it could soon lead the clean energy transition in many more ways than that.
